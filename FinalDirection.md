Final Concept: 

Name: Jumpscare

Input: How far the user is from the screen

Overview: The user will start somewhat far away from the screen. There will be something very cute and small on the screen (have not decided what that will be yet) that is very small and hard to see. There will also be the words 'come closer' on the screen. The user will then get closer to the screen and when they get to a certain distance from the camera, there will be a scary creature that will pop out from behind their shoulder to jumpscare them. The screen will also flash a bright light and play a screaming sound to add to the shock effect.
